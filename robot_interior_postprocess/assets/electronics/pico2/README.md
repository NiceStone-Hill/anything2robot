# Raspberry Pi Pico 2, no headers

Official Raspberry Pi STEP from the [product portal](https://pip.raspberrypi.com/categories/1005-raspberry-pi-pico-2). Original archive member: `rpi_pico2.step`. Download metadata and SHA256 are in `source.json`; original MIT licence is preserved in `LICENSE.txt`.

`pico2.ply` is a topology-preserving triangulation for the offline viewer, in the STEP's original millimetres. Native bounds are approximately `[-10.5,-25.5,-0.04]` to `[10.5,26.8,3.69]` mm, including USB overhang. The preview uses an assigned green display colour; material colours are not measured product properties.

The configuration rotates the board in its tray and raises its underside by 1 mm. It never scales the board to fill the former Uno envelope. The `60 × 30 × 20 mm` Pico planning envelope includes provisional wiring/USB allowance. Tray attachment, USB insertion and soldered lead retention remain unverified.
